## inspiration
a day made of glass 2 (2012) was the inspiration for building this web app. in it, one of the main characters is on a school field trip to what seems to be a national park. there are massive walls of interactive glass to learn about dinosaurs who thrived several thousand years ago in the exact location she is at. as a child, when i watched the vision of corning glass, i remember wishing it become a reality in that instance. that thought has stuck with me over the years. 

## what it does
seekh is a simplified web app intended to be viewed by children that showcases a timeline of dinosaurs spanning from the triassic to the cretaceous period. users can scroll horizontally to see a chronological timeline of dinosaurs. 

## how i built it
nextjs, tailwindcss, framer motion, minimax ai and cursor ide
https://dinosaurpictures.org/ database

## challenges i ran into
styling took a while since the images were not able to expand to take up the entire div - i ran out of time before i could complete the styling

## accomplishments that i'm proud of
parsing the dino db to retrieve names, pictures and summary of each dinosaur and displaying them in a chronological timeline

## what i learned
